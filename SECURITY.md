# Security

If you find a security problem in anything Wira Systems makes, please tell us privately and give us time to fix it before anyone else hears of it.

- **Do not open a public issue,** and do not post it anywhere public.
- **Report it privately** through GitHub's *Report a vulnerability* on the repository's Security tab, or, for a product you use, through the contact on its About page.
- **Say what you found,** how to reproduce it, and what it could let someone do.

We will answer, keep you told while we fix it, and thank you when the fix is out, unless you would rather not be named.

Please never test on a real shop's data or devices without its owner's permission.
